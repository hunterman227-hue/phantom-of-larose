# Phantom of LaRose

2026 Mayflower quarter die progression research, under the Twisted Penny / Forensic Series brand.

## The project

A forensic photo audit of the 2026 Mayflower quarter (Philadelphia and Denver strikes),
tracking die markers, die cracks, and a recurring bird-like figure — the Phantom of LaRose —
that forms at the front of the ship's mast as the die deteriorates.

## The Phantom rule

A coin is the Phantom of LaRose only if it carries ALL five die markers:

1. **Splinter** — metal displacement on the pinky
2. **Mosquito Bite** — on the lady pilgrim's arm
3. **Elbow anomaly**
4. **Blurry letters / face**
5. **Anchor**

The full Phantom appears only at the end of the progression, once every marker has shown up.

Named forms: Duck Head → Half Phantom → Full Phantom.

## Status

Private research repository. Nothing here is a confirmed attribution or a for-sale
listing. Photographed coins are documented as observed; composite crack maps are
testing maps collected across multiple specimens, not proof of a single working die.
