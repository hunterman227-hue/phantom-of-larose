# Theory rundown — September 17, 2026

Three theories on the table for the 2026-P Mayflower quarter die events. Written at the user's request.

## 1. Pete Apple — die flow waves
The marks are die flow waves: metal flow lines in the die from the striking process. Explicitly NOT spalling, ghosting, clash, feeder finger damage, PIDT, or over-tonnage. Interesting coins, but no dollar value in his view.

## 2. Randy Orre — bi-level die cracks (brittle fracture)
The cracks are bi-level die cracks: not plain raised lines but cracks with a step, where the die face sank slightly on one side. Brittle fracture in nature. Cause: complex modern designs on hard-metal coins like quarters create multi-directional stress as planchet metal flows into the relief, stressing the die early in its life. Recommends AmScope trinocular microscope for examination. Sees possible feeder finger marks in one photo but says focus on the bi-level cracks.

## 3. David James Jr. (the user) — pressure mirroring
The striking pressure is running so high that the cracks on the front (obverse) of the coin are mirroring the reverse. The metal is driven so hard it carries the reverse design through, and that force is what cracks the die on the front side.
