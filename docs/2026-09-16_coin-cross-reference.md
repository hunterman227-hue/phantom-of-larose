## Headline findings

- **38 of 40 files analyzable** (2 non-diagnostic: 017 blurry screen, 021 monitor moiré). 35 unique images (5 byte-identical duplicate pairs).
- **Obverse Die-1 markers:** raised dots and a wavy raised ridge near the pilgrim's hand/arm are real, repeatable features across 10 photos (001–010, 016) — high confidence the features exist; mechanism (die chip vs. die-flow waves vs. other) remains unproven and provisional. No published variety matches them.
- **Phantom stages:** only three photos show anything in the front-of-mast/foresail zone worth noting — 011 and 013 (possible early/duck-head-stage small raised feature; low-medium confidence) and 014 (possible middle-crack-stage linear raised feature; low-medium confidence). No half-phantom or full-phantom stage is visible in any photo at usable magnification. All D-mint reverses (20+ coins across group and single shots) show normal strikes with no Phantom features at visible magnification.
- **Published varieties:** none — no CONECA, Variety Vista, or Wexler listing for any 2026 Mayflower quarter die variety found (2026-09-16 web check). The only published error-market activity is struck-through error commerce (Littleton "Spirit of '76" set; eBay listings), which is not a die-variety listing.
