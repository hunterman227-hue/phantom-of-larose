# Phantom of LaRose — Three-Day Treasure Hunt

## The idea

Three days. Each day you reveal one leg of the voyage on the treasure map and drop one clue. The audience follows the Mayflower from the storm to the swamp. On the third day, X marks the phantom, but the phantom only appears for hunters carrying every marker: the anchor, the die chips, and the cracks.

## The map

Show the treasure map a piece at a time. Day one shows the top of the map: the ship, the lightning, the dotted route veering off. Day two shows the middle: Bayou Lafourche, the cypress knee hazard, the hull crack. Day three shows the whole map with the red X at LaRose and the words X marks the phantom.

## Day one: the storm

Tell the voyage and the lightning. The Mayflower leaves, the storm takes her, the bolt strikes the mast by the U and tears down the front of the mast, and she veers off course, lost.

Clue one: "I ride at the front of the boat and I never leave. I hold fast through the storm, the bayou, and the swamp. What am I?"

Answer: the anchor. The anchor is the first marker, and it stays visible through the whole hunt.

## Day two: the bayou

Tell the bayou leg. The lost ship finds Bayou Lafourche where Jean Lafitte comes up, lands at LaRose, strikes the cypress knee, and the hull splits with a crack straight down the middle. The jerk throws the man into the mast and tears the lady's bonnet.

Clue two: "The knee in the water splits the wood. What runs straight down the middle of the boat?"

Answer: the hull crack. Markers collected so far: the anchor, the hull crack, the bonnet crack, the head strike.

## Day three: the swamp

Tell the swamp leg. The man climbs out and takes the splinter in his left pinky. The snake bites both his feet: the two die chips under the two and the six. He falls, strikes his head, and the world goes blurry: the blurred letters and blurred faces. He puts his arm around the lady, the mosquito lands, she slaps it: the mosquito bite. The swamp branches whip thin lines across the nose.

Clue three: "Two bites under the two and the six. What left them?"

Answer: the snake. Then lay out the full marker roll call: anchor, splinter, mosquito bite, elbow anomaly, blurry letters and face, bonnet crack, nose cracks, hull crack, snake bites, head strike.

## The finale

Reveal the full map with the red X. X marks the phantom. The phantom has been materializing on the back of the boat the whole time: duck head, then half phantom, then the full Phantom of LaRose. Only a hunter carrying every single marker reaches the X. Anyone missing one does not get the phantom.

## How the audience plays

Each day, hunters comment their answer to the clue. On day three, they list every marker they collected. You read the roll call on the show and crown the hunters who carry the full set. The hunt can run across TikTok, YouTube, and the Whatnot show, with the finale live.
