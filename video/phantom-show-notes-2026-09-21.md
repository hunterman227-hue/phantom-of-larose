# Phantom of LaRose — Whatnot Show Notes
Monday, September 21, 2026 — on Whatnot

## Whatnot is a live auction app
People come to Whatnot to buy during the show. That fits your "might sell something" plan — you can run the show as pure story and still auction a few items at the end, or skip selling entirely. Either way you need to be approved as a seller before you can go live.

## Setup checklist (do today, before tomorrow)
1. Get the Whatnot app and create your account. You must be 18 or older.
2. Apply to become a seller inside the app. It takes about 5 to 7 minutes. They ask about your selling experience and what you plan to sell — answer honestly and in detail. You need their approval before you can host, so do this today.
3. Once approved, connect your bank account through Stripe so you can get paid.
4. Set your shipping preferences in the Seller Hub before the show.
5. Schedule the show: tap the plus icon, choose Schedule a Show. Give it a clear title, upload the thumbnail (already made for you), pick the Coins category, and set shipping for the show.
6. Preload your items into the show with photos and starting prices so buyers can preview them.
7. Try Rehearsal Mode first — it lets you practice going live with no viewers so you can learn the buttons.

## Show rundown (in order)
1. Cold open — play the intro video (35 seconds). Let it run start to finish, no talking over it.
2. Welcome — Twisted Penny, Forensic Series. You are tracking a die variety on the 2026 Mayflower quarter, and the variant was found on the bayou. Tonight you are laying out the whole treasure map, auditing the coins pulled off the bayou one by one.
3. Opening giveaway — run a small quick giveaway in the first few minutes. It pulls viewers into the room and the app rewards high viewer counts.
4. Tell the bayou story — the Mayflower comes up the river toward the bayou. Lightning strikes the mast and the mast comes down, but it does not hit the pilgrim. He gets out of the boat. As the woman gets out, the snake bites her on the foot and then on the leg while he holds her and helps her out — those two bites are the two and the six die chips. Getting out, he puts his left hand on the boat and gets the splinter in his left pinky. The cypress knee hits the hull on the way into the bayou and cracks it. The anchor rides on the front of the mast the whole time. And through all of it, the phantom is growing in the background.
5. The markers — hold each coin close to the camera and walk through them one by one: the mosquito bite on her arm, the splinter on the pinky, the ant bite, the elbow, the blurry letters and blurry face, the anchor. Slow down here. Name each one and say where it sits on the coin.
6. The rule — a coin is only the Phantom of LaRose if it carries ALL of the markers. The phantom grows in stages: duck head, then half phantom, then the full Phantom of LaRose at the end of the treasure map.
7. Progression sets — four coins, one phantom. Collect all four coins. Coming soon.
8. Auctions (optional) — only if you want to sell. Keep each item fast: describe it, show its condition to the camera, start the auction, move on. Do not spend ten minutes on one coin.
9. Close — recap the rule in one sentence, say when the next show is, tell people where to follow: your Phantom of LaRose Facebook page and your YouTube page.

## Reminders for during the show
- Phone on a tripod, good lighting, clean background behind you.
- Hold each coin close to the camera and steady for a few seconds on each marker.
- Read the comments out loud when you answer them so everyone follows along.
- Keep the energy up and the pace moving — describe, show, auction, next.
- If tech glitches, keep talking the story — the story is the show.
